My research findings on the digital divide

The digital divide is a very big issue and my research has proven that:

  • It has gotten worse over the last 6 years especially during covid time
  • Access to good quality internet matters as people in North Wales don't have good quality internet due to lack of fibre cables
  • The gap between the wealthy and the underpriviledged is actively increasing. Although technology is getting better there is still a massive divide between both classes
