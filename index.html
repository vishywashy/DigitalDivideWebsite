My research findings on the digital divide

What is the digital divide?

The digital divide is defined as the divide between technology and lack of access to information between underprivileged people and wealthy households

I have found out that the most important factor of the digital divide comes down to digital literacy.

The digital divide is a very big issue and my research has proven that:

  • It has gotten worse over the last 6 years especially during covid time
  • Access to good quality internet matters as people in the North don't have good quality internet due to lack of fibre cables for example North Wales
  • The gap between the wealthy and the underprivileged is actively increasing. Although technology is getting better there is still a massive divide between both classes

What can we do to close the gap between the wealthy and the underprivileged?

The government is actively rolling out broadband and optical fibre to rural towns in North Wales and parts of South West Wales to try and connect everyone to the internet

Although, this will already help a lot of people who weren't able to stay connected to the internet. There is still a small number of people that are unable to have access to the internet.

This is because poverty is a big issue as a result they can't afford new devices as a result they will need some grants or support from the government.

To help decrease the gap you can donate to these communities so that they can provide new devices and training to rural people improving their computer literacy hence decreasing the digital divide.
